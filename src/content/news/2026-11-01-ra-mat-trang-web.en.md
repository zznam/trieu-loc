---
title: Launching the Triệu Lộc hometown website
date: 2026-11-01
summary: A community website for locals, people living far from home, and visitors who want to learn about Triệu Lộc.
---

**Triệu Lộc — Homeland of Lady Triệu** is now live. It aims to preserve the commune's history and culture and to connect people at home and abroad.

On the site you can:

- Learn the history of the land and the life of Lady Triệu
- Browse a map of relics and landmarks
- Discover local food and a directory of local businesses
- Follow commune news and events

Everyone is welcome to send photos, stories and business details to help build the site.

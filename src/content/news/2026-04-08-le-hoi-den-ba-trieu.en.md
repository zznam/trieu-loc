Every year from the **21st to the 24th of the 2nd lunar month**, the Bà Triệu special national monument in Phú Điền village welcomes tens of thousands of visitors who come to honour the heroine Triệu Thị Trinh.

The ceremonies include rites by female officiants and court-style rites, a palanquin procession from the temple to Phú Điền communal house, and offerings at the tomb on Mount Tùng. Festivities include a staged re-enactment of the uprising of 248 and traditional folk games.

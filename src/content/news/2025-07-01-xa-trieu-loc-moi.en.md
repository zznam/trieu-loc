---
title: The new Triệu Lộc commune begins operating
date: 2025-07-01
summary: From 1 July 2025, the new Triệu Lộc commune was formed by combining Đại Lộc, Tiến Lộc and Triệu Lộc.
---

Under Resolution 1686/NQ-UBTVQH15 of the National Assembly Standing Committee on reorganising commune-level units in Thanh Hóa, the entire area and population of **Đại Lộc, Tiến Lộc and Triệu Lộc** communes were combined into a new commune named **Triệu Lộc**.

The new commune covers about 29.27 km², with over 26,000 residents in 17 villages, and sits directly under Thanh Hóa province now that the district level has been abolished.

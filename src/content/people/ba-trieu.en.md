Triệu Thị Trinh, revered as **Lady Triệu** or the **Lady King**, raised a revolt with her brother Triệu Quốc Đạt against Eastern Wu rule.

After many battles the uprising was crushed, and she died on Mount Tùng in today's Triệu Lộc. Her tomb on Mount Tùng and the temple at the foot of Mount Gai together form the Bà Triệu special national monument.

Her famous words have been handed down for generations:

> “I want to ride the strong wind, tread the fierce waves, slay the great whale of the Eastern Sea, drive out the Wu army, reclaim our land and throw off the yoke of slavery — I will not bow my back to be anyone's concubine.”
